What to pack

Waterproof coat, comfortable shoes, warm knitwear, gloves and scarf. Take grippy footwear for the Giant's Causeway and extra layers for Twickenham and any open-top bus tour.

Dress-up days

30 December: change at the Airbnb before afternoon tea at The Wolseley. 31 December: smart-casual NYE outfit. 2 January: smart comfortable clothes or subtle Peaky Blinders-inspired style.
