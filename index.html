London · Battersea
29 December – 4 January · 6 nights

Our Battersea Airbnb

Family accommodation in Battersea, south of the Thames, with Battersea Park, riverside neighbourhoods and good connections to central London. Check-in is planned from 15:00 on 29 December. The exact address is shared privately.

Photo: Battersea Power Station neighbourhood landmark — not the Airbnb property.

☕ Coffee & breakfast

WatchHouse at Battersea Power Station, Dishoom breakfast naans and cafés around Battersea Square. Local coffee options may have reduced holiday hours.

🍴 Food nearby

Dishoom Battersea for sharing plates, Arcade Battersea for plenty of choices and Fiume for Italian riverside dining. All remain dinner options on arrival day.

🍸 Drinks & pubs

Control Room B: confirmed table for five, 29 December 18:00–20:00. The Prince Albert: preferred New Year's Eve venue but still awaiting confirmation. The Surprise in Chelsea is another pub option.

🌳 Nearby highlights

Battersea Park, Battersea Power Station, riverside walks, Lift 109 and festive installations. MG&Co. / Matilda Goad in Belgravia is a priority shopping detour on 29 December, timing permitting.

🏛️ Sights & connections

Before you go

Helpful information

What to pack

Waterproof coat, comfortable shoes, warm knitwear, gloves and scarf. Take grippy footwear for the Giant's Causeway and extra layers for Twickenham and any open-top bus tour.

Dress-up days

30 December: change at the Airbnb before afternoon tea at The Wolseley. 31 December: smart-casual NYE outfit. 2 January: smart comfortable clothes or subtle Peaky Blinders-inspired style.

Places to explore

Covent Garden, Soho, Southwark and Shoreditch have optional cafés, shops, pubs and restaurants embedded in the daily itinerary.
